I went out to buy dinner and took the long way back. There was no good reason. The air had cooled down, and I did not feel like going indoors yet.

Near the water, someone was trying to get a small dog to look at the camera. The dog was interested in everything except the camera. I watched for a while, pretending to be interested in the view, and then realized I had taken several photographs of almost exactly the same patch of sky.

The color never comes out properly on my phone. In person it was a pale pink fading into grey. In the photographs it looked like an ordinary cloudy evening. I kept them anyway.

I had been in an irritable mood all afternoon. Nothing especially bad had happened. A few things took longer than expected, someone sent a message I did not know how to answer, and I kept opening another tab instead of finishing what was in front of me. By five o'clock I was annoyed with the tabs as well.

Outside, I was mostly thinking about whether I had ordered enough food.

That was quite nice.

On the way back I stopped at a little shop and bought something sweet that I had not intended to buy. A friend sent a photograph of a disastrous-looking first attempt at baking. We spent a few minutes discussing whether it was burnt or simply “deeply caramelized.” I have no qualifications to judge, but I voted for the second description.

By the time I got home, dinner was cold. I ate it while looking through the photographs and deleted all but three, then restored one because a tiny person on a bicycle had accidentally made the composition better.

I would like to say I returned refreshed and had a wonderfully productive evening. Actually, I watched half an episode of something, washed a bowl, and went to bed earlier than usual.

The remaining photographs are still in my phone. Four almost identical skies, if I am being honest.
