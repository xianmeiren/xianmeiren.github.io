## About Me

I am Xianmei Ren (任宪美), a Ph.D. candidate in Statistics at Zhejiang University of Finance & Economics, with an expected graduation date of June 2027.

My research focuses on combinatorial optimization, particularly shop scheduling. My current work centers on manufacturing applications, including digital-twin-supported scheduling, alongside collaborative research in logistics. I develop mathematical models and optimization algorithms, drawing on evolutionary computation and reinforcement learning.

Before my doctoral studies, I worked as a data engineer in the manufacturing industry for nearly three years. This experience shapes my interest in mathematical models that capture operational constraints and optimization methods for scheduling under changing conditions.

I am interested in extending this work to related combinatorial optimization problems and welcome opportunities for collaborative research.

## Research Interests

- **Combinatorial optimization and scheduling:** Mathematical modeling and optimization for shop scheduling in manufacturing, with related applications in transportation and logistics.
- **Evolutionary computation:** Evolutionary algorithms and swarm intelligence for complex optimization problems.
- **Reinforcement learning and digital twins:** Learning-assisted optimization and digital-twin-supported scheduling under changing operational conditions.
