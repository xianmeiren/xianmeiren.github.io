## CMEIM 2026 · Online · May 15, 2026

I participated in the **3rd International Conference on Materials Engineering & Intelligent Manufacturing (CMEIM 2026)** and gave an oral presentation titled **“Digital Twin-Driven Dynamic Scheduling of Flexible Job Shops” (数字孪生驱动的柔性作业车间动态调度)**.

The conference was hosted by Northwestern Polytechnical University and held online. Its keynote talks and oral sessions brought together work on materials engineering and intelligent manufacturing, including manufacturing optimization, additive manufacturing, and structural design.

## Presentation Focus

My presentation addressed dynamic scheduling in flexible job shops from the perspective of Industry 5.0. It introduced the role of digital twins in connecting production states with scheduling decisions and discussed why worker factors belong in the optimization of intelligent manufacturing systems.

- **Digital twin support:** Connecting an evolving production environment with a representation that can support decision-making.
- **Worker factors:** Considering people alongside equipment when studying feasible and adaptable schedules.
- **Manufacturing optimization:** Exploring how dynamic scheduling can respond to changing conditions on the shop floor.
